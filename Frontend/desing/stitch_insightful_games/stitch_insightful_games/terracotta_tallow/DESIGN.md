## Brand & Style

This design system reimagines tactical gaming through an earthy, bohemian, and artisanal lens—rejecting standard tropes of harsh neon lines, dark mode military interfaces, and clinical sci-fi displays. Instead, it adopts the material world of field naturalists, cartographers, desert navigators, and tactile tabletop strategists.

### Aesthetic Ethos
- **Warm Tactile Organicism:** Interfaces evoke hand-milled rag paper, sun-dried terracotta tiles, woven linen sheets, and warm clay tablets. Every surface possesses visual weight and natural warmth.
- **Expansive Breathability:** Tactical clarity emerges through deliberate negative space, natural sunlight tones, and uncluttered composition rather than dense grids of chrome and data.
- **Artisan Precision:** Information architecture remains rigorously functional for strategic management while retaining handcrafted warmth—gentle deckled radii, soft debossed surface transitions, and unhurried editorial flow.
- **Target Audience:** Players and strategists drawn to narrative depth, slow-burn exploration, botanical field-craft, and worldbuilding that values craft, ritual, and natural topography over mechanized warfare.

## Colors

The palette balances sun-baked mineral pigments with raw unbleached fibers and vegetal tones, generating high readability while preserving an organic sanctuary feel.

### Surface System
- **Base Canvas (`#f7f3eb`):** Washed linen tint used for root-level screens, vast canvas backdrops, and map terrains.
- **Surface Elevation 1 (`#f0ebd9`):** Natural sun-dried paper tier for interactive panels, contextual drawers, and tactical cards.
- **Surface Elevation 2 (`#e8dfcf`):** Raw oat fiber tier for inset modules, countersunk weapon matrices, and pressed inventory pockets.

### Chromatic Accents
- **Primary Terracotta (`#d85a2b` / `#e05326`):** The hearth fire of the interface. Reserved for decisive tactical commands, primary phase actions, focus rings, and high-impact unit stats.
- **Secondary Olive & Sage (`#78866b`):** The living brush. Utilized for tactical terrain modifiers, non-combative movement zones, stamina gauges, and support abilities.
- **Tertiary Ochre & Caramel (`#a77b38` / `#8c6023`):** Sunned earth and leather binding. Used for lore tags, squad ranks, historical chronicles, resource tallies, and secondary badges.
- **Deep Espresso Charcoal (`#231f1e` / `#2c2523`):** Natural charcoal stick ink for foundational typography, tactical iconography, and grounding dividers. Replaces synthetic `#000000` across all contexts.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs the literary authority of **Playfair Display** with the clear, rounded, and approachable cadence of **Plus Jakarta Sans**. 

- **Headlines (Playfair Display):** Conveys narrative weight and geographic majesty. It functions as an illuminated manuscript header, framing campaign phases, tactical zone manifests, and unit titling with classical gravitas.
- **Body & Labels (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Balances modern legibility with soft humanist curvature that prevents statistical tables and combat logs from feeling synthetic or utilitarian.
- **Editorial Balance:** Maintain loose, generous line-heights on body copy to allow natural cream background light to filter through passages. Labels maintain a subtle tracking expansion (+0.02em to +0.05em) when set in uppercase tactical tiers.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts eschew rigid HUD boundaries in favor of a breathable, multi-tiered fluid canvas modeled on expedition map tables and naturalist journals.

### Grid & Composition
- **Desktop (12-Column Fluid):** Margin set to `3rem` with `1.5rem` gutters. Interactive action matrices align into organic cards that float effortlessly above the parchment canvas.
- **Tablet (8-Column Fluid):** Margins contract to `2rem`, preserving generous internal component padding over border boundaries.
- **Mobile (4-Column Fluid):** Margins set to `1.25rem` with `1rem` gutters. Deeply inset sidebars reflow into slide-up linen field sheets.

### Spacing Philosophy
- Ample negative space provides tactical breathing room: unit profiles, status gauges, and gear rosters never press against their container boundaries.
- Spacing relies on multiples of the `0.75rem` / `1.25rem` progression to evoke relaxed human measurements rather than calculated digital divisions.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy eschews drop-shadows with harsh black alphas and cold volumetric bevels. Depth is articulated through **tonal bedding** and **sun-filtered warm ambient shadows**.

### Depth Layers
1. **Parchment Bedding (Layer 0):** Unraised `#f7f3eb` base layer. Non-interactive backdrop and structural negative space.
2. **Resting Field Sheets (Layer 1):** Tone shifts to `#f0ebd9` augmented with a warm, low-slung ambient shadow (`box-shadow: 0 4px 16px -2px rgba(76, 56, 36, 0.08), 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(76, 56, 36, 0.04)`). Card surfaces appear like hand-cut paper pressed onto a work surface.
3. **Elevated Tactical Overlays (Layer 2):** Flyouts, floating battle banners, and active piece focus states lift upwards with warm amber-tinted dispersion (`box-shadow: 0 12px 32px -4px rgba(76, 56, 36, 0.12), 0 4px 8px -2px rgba(76, 56, 36, 0.06)`).
4. **Countersunk Wells (Inset):** Sub-inventories, empty relic sockets, and progress troughs employ subtle inner shade (`box-shadow: inset 0 2px 4px 0 rgba(44, 37, 35, 0.06)`) on background `#e8dfcf`.

## Shapes

The geometric signature is defined by softened organic contours—reminiscent of sea-smoothed clay, tumbled stones, and rounded edge-bindings.

- **Standard Base Radii (`0.5rem` / `8px`):** Applied to badges, inputs, combat stat chips, and button controls. Corners feel softened by human hands without sliding into novelty circles.
- **Structural Container Radii (`1rem` / `16px`):** Used for tactical cards, floating expedition dockets, map overlays, and modal dialogues.
- **Organic Accents:** Large layout cards and heroic callouts can employ asymmetric radii (e.g., `1.5rem 0.75rem 1.5rem 0.75rem`) to echo hand-crafted leather-bound field guides and raw terracotta tiles.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Advance, Deploy, Cast):** Solid `#d85a2b` terracotta fill with `#f7f3eb` linen text. Warm glow hover (`rgba(216, 90, 43, 0.25)` expansion), depressed tactile state shifting `1px` downward.
- **Secondary Action (Maneuver, Inspect):** Tinted linen base (`#f0ebd9`) framed by a soft ochre hairline border (`1px solid #a77b38/40`). Espresso text (`#231f1e`).
- **Support / Natural Action (Heal, Camouflage):** Soft olive tone (`#78866b`) with warm linen text.

### Chips & Tactical Tokens
- Rounded compact capsules featuring `12px` font sizing.
- Categorical chips use muted vegetal washes: Olive backgrounds for terrain attributes, Ochre for rank modifiers, and Terracotta for turn priorities. Subtle `1px` tinted borders seal the contours.

### Tactical Cards
- Grounded in `#f0ebd9` with an ultra-fine border tone (`rgba(167, 123, 56, 0.2)`).
- Headers sport **Playfair Display** in `#231f1e` with generous `space-md` internal padding. Dividers use organic dotted or hairline separators rendered in softened ochre rather than flat gray.

### Input Fields
- Recessed `#e8dfcf` tone with countersunk ambient depth.
- Border shifts on focus from a quiet bone tone to a bold terracotta line (`#d85a2b`) supported by a diffused `3px` ambient halo (`rgba(216, 90, 43, 0.15)`).

### Selection Controls (Checkboxes & Radios)
- Custom ceramic-pip design. Inactive states feature unbleached cream squares/circles bordered by `#8c6023`.
- Active states flood with `#d85a2b` featuring a softened linen check or warm ochre central bead.

### Domain-Specific Components
- **Turn Wheel / Compass Rosette:** Botanical tactical wheel rotating around a terracotta hub, displaying upcoming moves and weather tides.
- **Relic Docket:** Tabular unit status interface resembling a field naturalist's sketchbook, utilizing faint horizontal ruling and dry-pressed stamp badges.
